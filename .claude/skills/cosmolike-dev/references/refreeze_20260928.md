# The 2026-09-28 refreeze: non-Limber C_gg growth fix

Dated development history, removed from the project test READMEs (they
document the current suite; this record explains why every frozen
chi2 reference changed on 2026-09-28).

## The defect

Below l = 150 cosmolike's `C_cl_tomo` computes

    C_l = C_l^FFTLog(P_lin) + C_l^Limber(P_delta) - C_l^Limber(P_lin),

an exact FFTLog projection of the linear power spectrum plus, in the
Limber approximation, what linear theory misses. Where the Limber
approximation holds (high l) the first and third terms must converge
to the same number and cancel, leaving the Limber C_gg. The FFTLog
term needs separable growth, D(z1) D(z2) P_lin(k, z=0), but the
subtracted Limber term used CAMB's P_lin(k, z), whose growth depends
on scale with massive neutrinos: the two differ by 0.7% at z = 0.3 and
1.6% at z = 1, so the pair never cancelled. Every C_gg below the
switch to Limber carried a -0.85% to -1.6% offset, and the 1% early
exit fired near l = 50, where the decaying non-Limber correction
happened to cancel the offset, dropping a real 1.3% to 2.2% correction
for the highest lens bins. With the fix the subtracted term uses
D(a)^2 P_lin(k, z=0), and the two terms agree to 0.02% to 0.17% at
l = 149. The fix changes w(theta) only; cosmic shear and
galaxy-galaxy lensing are unchanged.

On 2026-09-28 the fix landed, the shipped data vectors were
regenerated with the fixed code, the galaxy-galaxy lensing default
switched to non-Limber (`adopt_limber_gs: 0`), and every project
snapshot was refrozen; the regenerated vectors' chi2 values became the
new references, recorded in each `frozen/reference_chi2.json` `_meta`
block.

## The current implementation

`C_cl_tomo` now anchors the separable spectrum per lens bin instead of
at z = 0: (D(a)/D(a_piv))^2 P_lin(k, a_piv), with a_piv = 1/(1+zbar)
and zbar the bin's mean redshift, so the separable form is exact at
that redshift. The derivation and consequences live in the comments of
`C_cl_tomo` in `cosmolike/cosmo2D.c`; the related CCL-side study of
separable-growth offsets is in the CCL-benchmark repository.
