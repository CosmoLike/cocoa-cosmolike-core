# Complete survey covariance scaling (2026-10-04)

## Installed-library baseline

Build-provenance correction: the external runner unset lower-case project
names, but the skip-compilation flags are upper-case except for DES
cluster. Its build logs for roman_fourier, roman_kl and des_y3 were empty:
those projects used their previously installed libraries. The other four
projects did rebuild. The table remains a measurement of the binaries
actually run, but it is not a uniform Gaussian-only optimization baseline.
Repository commit IDs alone do not identify a loaded library's source.
The subsequent shared-block comparison rebuilds the affected interfaces
explicitly and retains the saved arrays as an exact arithmetic reference.

Apple M2 Pro, BLAS fixed at one thread. Each case has one excluded
warm-up per thread count and three complete matrix recomputations.
CAMB and initialization are excluded; core readers are warm, but the
covariance and its shared matter tables are rebuilt on every call.
The runs were sequential, with no competing numerical jobs. Ordinary
desktop services remained active. Values below are means in seconds.

These are boost-1 forecasts under the supported massless, zero-IA,
zero-magnification, zero-RSD Limber model with G + SSC + cNG. They are
not inference-accuracy settings. Each project supplies its own bins and
cuts. Both real and Fourier outputs are measured even when the project
usually uses one space. DES Y1 x Planck here covers galaxy/shear fields,
not a CMB covariance. DES cluster here is its galaxy/shear forecast;
the separate joint 6x2pt+N timing is in covariance_gaussian_scaling.md.

All G, SSC, cNG, total and mean arrays are bitwise identical across
1/2/4/8 threads. Every total matrix passed Cholesky after timing, without
jitter or clipping. These checks establish deterministic implementation
and positivity for the supplied cases, not convergence of Fisher errors.

The installed-library eight-thread scaling is poor in several cases;
do not attribute that to the new Gaussian wrapper in the three projects
with skipped builds. LSST's build was current: its real-space
shared matter tables take 4.2168/2.4830/1.5987/1.4358 s at 1/2/4/8
workers. Its final SSC/cNG projection instead takes
0.3017/0.3280/0.3557/0.5333 s. The latter repeatedly calls the small
weighted projection from Python; assess whole-block scheduling after
removing unused halo-moment work. Do not extrapolate Apple timings to
production x86 nodes.

The internal raw record is test/covariance_reference/results/
scaling_gaussian_blocks/, generated by benchmark_all_projects.py and
run_scaling_projects.py. JSON records retain resolved settings, commit
IDs, stage times and individual repetitions; NPZ files retain baseline
arrays for exact before/after comparisons. These external files are
development records, not public README prerequisites.
